Visa Information

Entering China

China currently offers unilateral visa-free entry for citizens of certain countries and a 240-hour transit visa-free policy. Official information can be found at the National Immigration Administration.

China’s entry facilitation policies for foreign nationals can be found here.

1. Unilateral visa-free policy

As of November 10, 2025, the countries covered by the 30-day unilateral visa exemption policy are:

Andorra, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, Malta, Monaco, Montenegro, the Netherlands, North Macedonia, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Russia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Australia, New Zealand, Bahrain, Brunei, Japan, Kuwait, Oman, Saudi Arabia, South Korea, Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Peru, Uruguay.

Details: official policy page.

2. Transit visa-free policy

Applies to transit travel: country A → China → country B, where A must be different from B. As of November 10, 2025, the countries covered by the 10-day transit visa-free policy include Russia, the United Kingdom, the United States, and others (see the official website).

Details: official policy page.

Visa and entry policies are subject to change. Please refer to the official website for the most up-to-date information.
